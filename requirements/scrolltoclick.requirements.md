# Requirement: Scroll To Click

**URL:** http://www.uitestingplayground.com/scrolltoclick

**Scenario:** Scroll down to a button that is initially out of view and click it.

## Steps

1. Navigate to the Scroll To Click page.
2. Scroll down the page until the "Case 1 — Page Scroll" target button (`#scrollTarget1`) is visible.
3. Click the button.

## Expected Result

- The button's text changes to "Clicked!".
- The button's CSS class changes to include `btn-success` (from `btn-primary`).
